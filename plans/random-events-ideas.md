## Event Ideas

### 1. **Tweet Goes Viral**
> *Your hot take on "why tests are just vibes" is blowing up. Ride the engagement wave!*

**Mechanic:** Reaction/timing — hearts, retweets, and reply icons float across the screen for 20s. Click the positive engagement (❤️, 🔁), avoid clicking the ratio attempts (💀). 

**Success:** Temporary **cred multiplier** based on net positive engagements. "Your follower count translates to mass exposure."

---

### 2. **npm install Speedrun**
> *Someone deleted node_modules. Again. Reinstall everything before the team notices.*

**Mechanic:** Click-race — packages appear in a terminal-style list. Click each one as fast as it appears. Missed packages just don't count (no penalty).

**Success:** Temp **passive LoC/sec boost** — "Your blazing-fast recovery inspires the team."

---

### 3. **Merge Conflict Showdown**
> *Git says you and an AI agent both edited the same file. Only one version survives.*

**Mechanic:** Quick decision — shown 4-6 "conflict blocks" with two code snippets each. Pick HEAD or THEIRS within 3s per block. Correct answers are obvious-ish (one is clearly better/funnier).

**Success:** Temp **LoC/click multiplier** — "Clean merge. Momentum maintained."

---

### 4. **Context Window Crisis**
> *Your AI assistant is running out of context. Compress the prompt before it forgets everything!*

**Mechanic:** Whack-a-mole — redundant words/phrases highlight in a fake prompt. Click to remove them within 15s. More removed = better.

**Success:** Temp **LoC/click efficiency boost** — "Leaner prompts, fatter outputs."

---

### 5. **Rubber Duck Debugging**
> *You're stuck. Time to explain the problem to the duck.*

**Mechanic:** Sequence memory — 5-7 "thought bubbles" appear briefly in order, then shuffle. Click them back in the correct sequence.

**Success:** One-time **LoC burst** + small temp multiplier — "The duck said nothing, but you understand everything now."

---

### 6. **Mechanical Keyboard Arrived**
> *Your new Keychron just landed. Time to test those tactile browns.*

**Mechanic:** Typing — type a short meme phrase (e.g., `console.log("it works on my machine")`) within a time limit. Accuracy matters more than speed.

**Success:** Temp **LoC/click boost** — "Clacky keys, happy fingers, peak productivity."

---

### 7. **Dependabot PR Flood**
> *Dependabot opened 23 PRs overnight. Approve the safe ones, close the breaking changes.*

**Mechanic:** Sorting/reaction — PRs scroll past. Green-labelled ones (patch, minor) = click to merge. Red-labelled (major breaking) = let pass. Wrong merges don't penalise, just don't count.

**Success:** Temp **delegation multiplier** — "Your agents can work with stable dependencies."

---

### 8. **Hallucination Hunt**
> *Your AI just mass-generated documentation. Some of it is... creative. Find the hallucinations.*

**Mechanic:** Spot-the-error — shown a doc block with 3-5 obvious nonsense statements (e.g., "This function returns a promise that resolves to a horse"). Click the hallucinated lines.

**Success:** Temp **cred boost** — "Your quality standards are noticed. Clients trust you more."

---

### 9. **Live Pair Programming**
> *A YouTuber invited you on stream. Don't fumble the bag.*

**Mechanic:** Pattern completion — shown a simple code pattern (e.g., `[1, 2, 4, 8, ?]` or `const [a, b] = [1, ?]`), pick the right answer from 3 options. 4-5 rounds, ~5s each.

**Success:** One-time **cash + cred combo** — "You just got mass exposure and a collab fee."

---

### 10. **The Vercel Build Queue**
> *Deploy is stuck behind 47 other builds. Skip the queue by correctly guessing which projects will fail.*

**Mechanic:** Prediction/pattern — shown 5 project cards with "vibes" (project names like `untitled-42`, `final-final-v3`, `test123`). Click the ones that'll fail (obvious joke names). 

**Success:** Temp **income multiplier** — "Your project deployed first. Client impressed."
